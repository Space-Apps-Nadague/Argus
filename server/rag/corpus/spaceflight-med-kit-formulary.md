## Acetazolamide (Carbonic Anhydrase Inhibitor — SANS / Elevated ICP)

Clinical unit for spaceflight intracranial pressure and neuro-ocular edema management (requires Flight Surgeon authorization or autonomous red-flag protocol):
- Indication: Symptomatic Spaceflight-Associated Neuro-Ocular Syndrome (SANS) with ONSD >5.9 mm, optic disc edema, or persistent visual field changes unresponsive to posture/LBNP countermeasures.
- Adult spaceflight dosage: 250 mg PO every 12 hours (500 mg/day total) with 500 mL water; reassess ONSD, IOP, and visual acuity at 48 hours.
- Contraindications & spaceflight precautions: Contraindicated in sulfonamide hypersensitivity, severe renal impairment, hyponatremia/hypokalemia, or history of calcium phosphate nephrolithiasis (renal stones are an elevated microgravity risk; maintain >2.5 L/day fluid intake).

## Ibuprofen & Acetaminophen (Analgesic / Anti-inflammatory — Headache & Spine Pain)

Clinical unit for spaceflight hypercapnic headache, microgravity spinal elongation back pain, and musculoskeletal strain:
- Ibuprofen dosage: 400 mg PO every 6–8 hours with food and 250 mL water (maximum 1,200 mg/24 hours for self-care; up to 2,400 mg/24 hours under Flight Surgeon order).
- Acetaminophen dosage: 500–650 mg PO every 6 hours as needed for headache or fever (maximum 3,000 mg/24 hours).
- Contraindications & spaceflight precautions: Avoid Ibuprofen in dehydration, gastrointestinal ulcer history, renal insufficiency, or concurrent anticoagulant/corticosteroid therapy. Do not exceed 3,000 mg/day Acetaminophen; verify cabin ppCO2 (<3.5 mmHg) before treating any spaceflight headache.

## Ondansetron (5-HT3 Antagonist — Space Motion Sickness & Radiation Nausea)

Clinical unit for acute neuro-vestibular space motion sickness or radiation prodromal nausea and vomiting:
- Indication: Acute nausea/vomiting impairing oral hydration or safe EVA/flight operations, or prophylaxis during elevated radiation events.
- Adult spaceflight dosage: 4 mg orally disintegrating tablet (ODT) placed on tongue every 8 hours as needed (maximum 16 mg/24 hours).
- Contraindications & spaceflight precautions: Contraindicated with congenital long QT syndrome or concurrent apomorphine; monitor ECG if combined with other QT-prolonging medications. Correct electrolyte and fluid deficits with oral rehydration salts.

## Melatonin (Circadian Phase Alignment & Sleep Efficiency)

Clinical unit for circadian misalignment and sleep-efficiency drift (`sleep_eff` <82%) in orbital or deep-space habitats:
- Indication: Circadian sleep phase delay, slam-shift docking schedules, or sleep efficiency below 82%.
- Adult spaceflight dosage: 0.5 mg to 3 mg PO taken 30–60 minutes prior to scheduled habitat sleep period, paired with 480 nm blue-depleted evening lighting.
- Contraindications & spaceflight precautions: Do not administer within 6 hours of critical piloting, EVA, or Psychomotor Vigilance Testing (PVT); avoid combining with sedative-hypnotics unless directed by the Flight Surgeon.

## Hydrocortisone 1% Cream & Loratadine (Cabin Contact Dermatitis & Rash)

Clinical unit for closed-habitat dermal irritation, fiberglass/regolith dust erythema, or allergic urticaria:
- Topical regimen: Apply thin layer of Hydrocortisone 1% cream to affected intact skin twice daily for up to 7 days after gentle cleansing.
- Oral antihistamine regimen: Loratadine 10 mg PO once daily (non-sedating; safe during flight duty).
- Contraindications & spaceflight precautions: Do not apply topical corticosteroids to suspected fungal/bacterial skin infections, open lacerations, or periorbital tissue; photograph lesion with a millimeter scale for Flight Surgeon visual triage before first application.
