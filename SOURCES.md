# Authoritative Reference Sources

This document catalogs official space agency data and research resources for ARGUS agents and contributors. Consult these sources when grounding health indicators, spaceflight hazards, clinical guidance, radiation monitoring, and telemetry thresholds in future tasks.

---

## NASA Data & Resources

- **[5 Hazards of Human Spaceflight](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/hazards/)**: Basic information from NASA's Human Research Program (HRP) on the main hazards that affect humans on space missions. Also provides links to detailed NASA articles and reports related to each specific hazard.
- **[Risks of Human Spaceflight](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/risks/)**: Basic information from NASA's Human Research Program (HRP) on the main risks faced by humans on space missions. Also provides links to detailed NASA articles and reports related to each specific risk.
- **[Ongoing HRP Research in Space](https://www.nasa.gov/hrp/ongoing-hrp-research/)**: Articles from ongoing research in NASA's Human Research Program.
- **[The Space Medicine Exploration Medical Condition List (NASA NTRS 20110008645)](https://ntrs.nasa.gov/api/citations/20110008645/downloads/20110008645.pdf)**: NASA Technical Reports Server (NTRS) document (Watkins, Barr, & Kerstman, 2011) detailing the Exploration Medical Condition List used for space medicine risk management and operational medical planning in human space exploration missions.
- **[NASA Life Sciences Portal (NLSP) — Experiments Explorer](https://nlsp.nasa.gov/explore?dataType=EXPERIMENTS&sortBy=dateAdded_desc&page=1&limit=25&view=table)**: NASA's central portal for discovering and accessing archived Human Research Program (HRP) and Life Sciences Data Archive (LSDA) spaceflight and ground-analog experiments, including Standard Measures and physiological/biological datasets.
- **[Lifetime Surveillance of Astronaut Health (LSAH) — NLSP](https://nlsp.nasa.gov/resources/lsah)**: NASA's longitudinal health and occupational medical surveillance program tracking astronaut health outcomes, clinical metrics, and long-term spaceflight effects across crewmembers' lifetimes.

## Space Agency Partner Resources

### CSA (Canadian Space Agency)

- **[Monitoring Radiation for Astronaut Health](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/news/articles/2026/2026-02-26-monitoring-radiation-astronaut-health.asp)**: Learn how the CSA is helping monitor space radiation to better protect astronaut health during long-duration missions. Highlights Canadian technologies and research used to measure radiation exposure in space and improve understanding of its effects on the human body.
- **[Why Do We Conduct Science Experiments in Space?](https://www.asc-csa.gc.ca/eng/iss/science/why-do-we-conduct-science-experiments-in-space.asp)**: Explains how the unique environment of space allows scientists to conduct experiments that cannot be performed on Earth, and how research aboard the International Space Station advances understanding of human health, technology, and the universe while supporting future exploration missions.
